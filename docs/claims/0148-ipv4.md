## Notes

**Why this card:** N3 made the guest understand ARP, but the frames are
still Ethernet-raw. IPv4 is the first INTERNET layer and ICMP echo is
the canonical first proof on top of it — a guest that answers an echo
request byte-exact (echoed identifier/sequence/payload, recomputed
one's-complement checksums) is genuinely on the internet layer. Every
pattern N4 needs is proven: bounded TX (N1), ethertype dispatch in the
polled drain (N3), bounded BSS state (the N3 table), the runner's
capture-thread packet inspection (`--net-arp-respond` — an ICMP answer
is the same shape), and the script1/script2 settle gate pattern.

**Observe, don't assume (claim-time questions):** (a) does the device
touch any IP/ICMP field (checksum offload)? — the N1/N2 observation says
no offloads are negotiated and the frames pass through raw; the gate's
byte-exact captures pin it. (b) the net device's used-buffer IRQ line
remains unobserved (the N2/N3 record) — drain stays polled.
